# Saved research data dictionary

All paths below are relative to the repository root. These files contain executed model outcomes, measured times, and their alignment/provenance records. They do not contain redistributed input waveforms or per-case source feature matrices. See [licenses and attribution](../DATA_LICENSES.md) for the distinction between original study outputs and upstream labels/identifiers, and [code execution](CODE_EXECUTION.md) for replay commands.

## Samples, alignment, and units

HAR systems use seeds **17, 29, and 43**. The official held-out set contains **2,947 windows from nine subjects**: 2, 4, 9, 10, 12, 13, 18, 20, and 24. The primary same-case risk/time cohort contains **900 windows**, exactly 100 from each of those subjects. `test_indices`, `subjects`, and `labels` in the three seeds' timing outcome files are identical. Selection used subject IDs and window indices, not outcome labels. The cohort selection record is `data/results/har_certified_test/locked_execution_v1/frozen_shared_timing_cohort_before_predictions.json`.

HAR `case`, `test_index`, and NPZ `test_indices` address the official test ordering; they are not new participant IDs. `subjects`/`subject` are UCI's released pseudonymous integer IDs. Labels are integer class positions 0–5, matching the six probability columns. Use row identifiers to join files, rather than JSONL line order: timed method order was randomized within each case and repetition. The 2,947-window results assess prediction quality on all test windows; complete processing time was measured on the shared 900-window cohort. Do not attach the 900-window latency to a different case population without stating that difference.

The Hydraulic confirmation contains **435 selected source cycles**, four class positions 0–3, and **48 contiguous-condition proxy segments**. `original_rows`/`original_row` map to the 2,205-cycle UCI archive; `external_position` is position in this 435-cycle confirmation. `proxy_segments`/`proxy_segment` identify study grouping proxies, not documented independent physical runs or new machines. Consequently, 48 is not an independently replicated machine sample size. No independent-run confidence interval is supplied for this confirmation.

`complete_ms`, `partial_ms`, `full_ms`, and all latency quantities with `_ms` are milliseconds. A complete timer starts with the released raw window/cycle resident in host memory and ends at its emitted probability. It includes selected feature operations, scaling, allocations, inference/bounds, certificate construction, routing, and any exact fallback. It excludes acquisition/transmission, UCI preprocessing/window formation, disk/archive loading, model fitting, JIT warm-up, reference/label checks, and serialization. Exact per-experiment exclusions remain in the frozen configurations. Separately instrumented component profiles are diagnostic measurements and must not be added to or substituted for the primary complete timers.

Each online case/method was called three times: HAR uses `rep` and Hydraulic uses `repetition`, both 0–2. Repeated calls and different seeded systems on the same subject are not independent subjects. The shared-subject bootstrap resamples the same nine HAR subjects across all three systems; it does not treat 2,700 system-window observations or 8,100 calls per method as independently recruited cases.

`s` in a path means one of 17, 29, or 43. `M=17` is the main method/tolerance axis, `N` the case axis, and the final probability axis is the class axis. Probabilities, certificates, tolerances, and times are stored as float64 unless stated otherwise.

The 21 specifications include main methods, one auxiliary schedule, and three stopping controls; interpret their order using `heldout_analysis.json` and the analysis code, not the 17-method NPZ axis. |

Main method identity requires **both** `method_names` and `deltas`: the first 15 rows are `tight_minimax`, `coordinate_minimax`, and `tight_midpoint`, each at tolerances 0, 0.01, 0.025, 0.05, and 0.1. The last two rows are `native_full` and `compiled_full`, whose NPZ tolerances are NaN. The primary certified tolerance is 0.025. Full methods have JSON `delta: null`; NaN or null is not a zero tolerance. The control names are `score_separation_argmax`, `fixed_prefix_mean_completion`, and `entropy_mean_completion`; their certificate/tolerance entries may be NaN/null and do not establish the main probability-loss certificate.

For an emitted probability vector `p` and observed class `y`, half-Brier loss is `0.5 * sum((p - one_hot(y))**2)`. Excess is the emitted loss minus the corresponding frozen full-reference loss. A positive excess is degradation relative to that reference, not the absolute prediction error. The certificate concerns the maximum such excess over **every possible class**, conditional on valid outer bounds; it is not a confidence interval for generalization risk. Full-reference probabilities appear as HAR plural `reference_probabilities` and Hydraulic singular `reference_probability` in NPZ; JSONL consistently uses the singular when that field is present.

## Call records and summaries

HAR `seed{s}/timing_case_results.jsonl` has **56,700 rows per seed**: 900 cases × 21 specifications × three calls. Fields are `seed`, `case`, `test_index`, `subject`, `rep`, `name`, `delta`, `canonical`, `kind`, `probability` (six floats), `certificate`, `blocks`, `fallback`, `collapsed`, `complete_ms`, with a `guarantee` description on stopping-control records. `canonical` and `kind` distinguish main specifications from the auxiliary schedule and stopping controls. `fallback` identifies the exact full-prediction route after unsuccessful partial stopping; `collapsed` identifies an exact collapse of the remaining uncertainty. A deliberately full baseline is identified by its method name, rather than interpreted as a failed partial attempt.

Hydraulic `case_results.jsonl` has **22,185 rows**: 435 cycles × 17 specifications × three calls. It uses `method` rather than HAR's `name`, and additionally stores `external_position`, `original_row`, `proxy_segment`, `label`, `implementation_variant`, `reference_probability`, `half_brier`, integer `accuracy`, `reference_loss_excess`, `max_every_label_excess`, `certificate_violation_signed`, and `tolerance_violation_signed`. The last two are maximum every-label excess minus the certificate or requested tolerance; a positive value indicates a violation, subject to the numerical-check tolerance documented in the frozen driver. Tolerance violation is null for full baselines.

`data/results/review2_latency_analysis/saved_output_v1/paired_call_data.csv` has 8,100 aligned call rows for the primary partial/full comparison, with `seed, case, subject, rep, blocks, fallback, partial_ms, full_ms, paired_increment_ms`. The increment is **partial minus full**, so negative values mean a faster partial call. `paired_case_data.csv` has per-case means/medians across the three calls and the auxiliary schedule comparison; these are case summaries, not additional measurements. `latency_analysis.json` and `empirical_cdf_quantiles.csv` provide raw-call tail readouts, while `repeat_median_sensitivity.json` supplies the separate repeat-median sensitivity. Keep the retained long full-call outlier and adverse P99 findings when reproducing these results. Lower average latency does not establish a hard real-time deadline.

Primary aggregate results are in `data/results/har_certified_test/locked_execution_v1/primary_readout.json`; pooled/shared-subject estimates are in `data/results/final_analysis/har_locked_v1/heldout_analysis.json`. Bootstrap repetitions are 10,000 and the saved seed is 7302026. Full-model quality comparisons use the repaired `har_reference_test/locked_metric_repair_v1` records. The earlier `har_reference_test/locked_execution_v1` partial execution is retained as a technical failure history, not used as the completed reference comparison. Full-model complete timings are separately recorded under `data/results/review2_full_model_timing/20261002T071701Z_1526924f/`.

## Fitted parameters, provenance, and history

`data/provenance/runtime_metadata.json` contains **40 metadata arrays**, each with role, dtype, shape, original cache/member digest, and JSON values. Roles are `fitted_parameter`, `source_identifier`, `partition_index`, or `operator_definition`. HAR includes six 153-coordinate fitted center/scale vectors, nine signal names, 153 feature names, 18 block widths, offsets/group coordinates, sampling/window constants, and training/test row/subject alignment. Its fitting/development indices address the separate 7,352-row official training ordering. Hydraulic contains 238-coordinate fitted center/scale vectors, fitting/development index arrays of lengths 907/223 addressing its 1,770-row frozen development pool, source cycle/group alignment, and 28-block operator definitions. The metadata contains **no per-case source feature matrix, waveform, or label array**. Original source cache hashes are retained without distributing those caches.

`data/checkpoints/` contains **13 models**: HAR HGB, RF, ET, and LR for each seed, plus one Hydraulic HGB at `data/checkpoints/compute/full_hgb_seed17.joblib`. The three HAR SVC checkpoints are excluded because their support vectors retain individual source-derived feature rows. Their saved predictions, quality metrics, measured costs, training/selection configurations, and code remain; checkpoint omission does not remove their comparator results. Refitting SVC requires locally obtained, licensed source inputs.

`data/provenance/source_inventory.json` records published source-file mappings, sizes, hashes, and evidence categories. Frozen configuration/lock/preparation records remain under `data/provenance` and the relevant result directories. Original machine paths inside byte-preserved historical records describe the executed layout; they are not public download locations or required portable defaults.

`data/history/selective_replanning/` retains the seven-source planning-cost failure; `data/history/compute_value/` retains the failed 500-case gate pilot and its selection/calibration records; `data/history/certified_processing/` retains earlier slow prototypes. Failed conformance, partial executions, repairs, component diagnostics, stricter tolerances, and losing controls also remain in the result tree. These records document exploratory decisions and failure boundaries. They are not silently promoted to frozen confirmatory successes.
